Touch version of pad_twin_aim_diagonal, in a 390x844 window: a new game,
then the stick held on a diagonal 30 frames and slid straight up without
lifting, for ten more. 65 frames.

Twin: pad_twin_aim_diagonal.

Layout at 390x844 (`love.window_layout` tall mode: pad rectangle at
(0, 486.75), scale 2.4375; `love.pad_layout.place`, half-open screen
rectangles), re-derived with LuaJIT over the two modules:

- A (292, 720): A is x 263.25-321.75, y 691.5-750.
- Back (160, 745): Back is x 131.625-190.125, y 715.875-774.375, inside
  the stick zone (x 0-195, y 486.75-779.25).
- Stick base (90, 640): in the stick zone, on no button; base 117
  (radius 58.5, dead zone 14.625). Pushed from it: (90, 580) Up (1),
  (90, 700) Down (2), (30, 640) Left (4), (130, 600) Up+Right (9), mask
  bits as `love.pad_stick` gives them.

Each finger has its own id, never reused here. Every frame starts with
`"pad"`, so the help-me-debug cut works as for the pad replays.

- f0-3: touch 1 on A, the revealing touch on the hidden pad: it presses
  nothing (twin idle); f4 lifted. On the title A chooses the focused row,
  "forgiving": a leaked press would start a forgiving game.
- f9: touch 2 on the stick base, its first frame: mask 0 (twin idle).
  f10: touch 2 at (90, 700), Down (twin Down). f11 lifted.
- f12-14: touch 3 on A (twin A x3): a new game on "reasonable". f15
  lifted; aim mode from f15.
- f18: touch 4 on the stick base, its first frame: mask 0 (twin idle).
- f19-48: touch 4 at (130, 600), Up+Right (twin Up+Right x30): camera
  (176, 196) -> (226, 146).
- f49-58: the same finger slid to (90, 580), Up (twin Up x10): the
  direction changes at once, camera (226, 126) after f58.
- f59: lifted; idle to f64.

Rules exercised: the reveal presses nothing; a stick touch's first frame
is mask 0; a diagonal holds both bits; a slid finger changes direction
without a release.

Negative scratch run: the twin with Up+Right already on f18 (no mask-0
first frame) ends with camera (228, 122) instead of (226, 126).

Its `.json.sha256` and `.json.end-state` are byte copies of
pad_twin_aim_diagonal's, never a baseline of this run: `make e2e` and `make
end-state` thus prove this touch run equal to the gamepad run, frame by
frame and in the end state, and `tools/e2e_limits.sh` fails if the two
`.sha256` drift apart (after a `make e2e-baseline`, for example).

Not proven here: the drawing of the pad (step 16); a resize; overlay
mode; the 5 + 6 chord (covered by the Lua tests).

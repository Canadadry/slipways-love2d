Pick "reasonable" on the title screen with the D-pad and X. 32 frames.

A new game knows no object, so it starts in plain aim mode (plan 12
step 17): f21 is idle (it was square out of jump mode until then).
Every capture frame is unchanged.

Each press lasts one frame and is followed by a release frame: a held
direction moves the focus once. Row colours below are read in the
framebuffer dump (make replay on the file cut after that frame), at
x=108 of each row: forgiving y53-61, reasonable y64-72, challenging
y75-83, tough y86-94.

- f0-9: idle while the rows slide in. After f9: forgiving in colour 12,
  the other three in 13.
- f10: Down. After f10: reasonable 12, the others 13.
- f12: Down. After f12: challenging 12, the others 13.
- f14: Right, nothing to the right. After f14: challenging still 12.
- f16: Up. After f16 and f17: reasonable 12, the others 13.
- f18: X on "reasonable", held through f20. f18-19 are the loading
  screen (black, "loading" in colour 7 in the bottom-right corner); f20
  generates the galaxy and draws the new game.
- f21-31: idle into the first month, in aim mode: the cursor is the
  screen centre (80, 60), marked by the colour-7 crosshair (x78-82 on
  y=60, y58-62 on x=80, the centre pixel undrawn). Pad mode draws no
  cursor sprite on any frame.

`make state` at f31: clock year 3401 month 1, difficulty reasonable,
credits 100, planets 90, colonies 0, slipways 0, structures 0, techs 0,
nodes 90.

The same file with X at f18 on each of the four rows gives `make state`
lines that differ only in the difficulty line (planets included). The
chosen difficulty also shows in the f17 dump above (reasonable is the
only colour-12 row when X is pressed). test_pad_nav checks the started
game's economy_score_difficulty().

Covers no keyboard_* scenario: keyboard_menus is covered by
pad_pause_restart (step 3.3).
